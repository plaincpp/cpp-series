What you learn

  • Predict the result of / and % on negative int values, and find a division by zero with UBSan.
  • Use the precedence and grouping rules to predict a result, and add parentheses to make the order clear.
  • Explain the usual arithmetic conversions, and tell why -1 < 1u is false.
  • Use && and || to stop an operation that is not safe, and find = in a condition with -Wall.
  • Compare ++i with i++, and tell why i++ + i++ is undefined behavior.
